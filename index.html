Alignora — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026  ·  Applies to the Alignora mobile app (Android and iOS)

Alignora is a team app for independent sales teams. It lets a company invite its members, share announcements and resources, keep track of onboarding, and message clients from the member's own phone. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Alignora is operated by VConekt LLC ("we", "us"). Each company that uses Alignora (your "organization") controls the member and client records inside its own workspace; we provide and host the app on its behalf.

1. Information we collect

What we do not collect: precise location (GPS), photos, microphone, SMS content, call logs, advertising identifiers, or any analytics for advertising. Alignora contains no ads and no third-party ad or tracking SDKs.

2. Permissions the app asks for

  • Contacts — optional; read on-device only, to pick message recipients.
  • Notifications — optional; to receive team announcements.
  • Internet — required; to connect to your organization's workspace.

The app does not request permission to send SMS. Messages open in your own messaging app and you press Send yourself.

3. How we use information

  • Provide the app's features and keep your account secure.
  • Notify you about announcements and reminders from your organization.
  • Email you when an admin approves your account.
  • Diagnose crashes and improve reliability.
  • Comply with law and enforce our terms.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising.

4. Who can see your information

  • Your organization's admins and your team leader can see your profile, approval status, activity and broadcast history, as part of running the team.
  • Other members of your organization can see your name, city and country on the team page.
  • Service providers that host and run the app on our behalf (below). They process data only under our instructions.
  • Authorities, if required by law.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Your session token is stored in your device's secure keystore. Database access is restricted per organization and per role with row-level security, so one organization can never read another's data. Backups of app data are excluded from Android and iOS device backups.

7. Retention

  • Account and workspace data: kept while your account is active or while your organization keeps you as a member.
  • Crash diagnostics: deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Push tokens: removed when the token stops working or you uninstall the app.
  • After deletion of your account, personal data is removed from live systems within 30 days and from backups within 90 days.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your name, city and country in the app.
  • Revoke the Contacts or Notifications permission at any time in your phone settings.
  • Delete your account: ask your organization's admin, or email us at the address below. We will delete your profile and associated data as described in Retention.
  • Access, correct or export your data, or object to processing: email us. Residents of the EU/UK, California and other regions with privacy laws have these rights under GDPR, CCPA and similar laws; we honor them for everyone.

9. Children

Alignora is intended for adults running or joining a sales team. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

Our providers host data in the United States. If you use the app from another country, your information is transferred there and protected by the provider's standard contractual safeguards.

11. Changes

We will post any changes to this page and update the effective date. Material changes will also be announced in the app.
